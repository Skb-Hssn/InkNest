# Appearance and accent colors

Open **Settings** and choose an **Accent color**: Forest (the default), Teal, Blue, Violet, Rose, Orange, Amber, or Graphite.

Colors update immediately and are remembered across app restarts. The accent applies to selected notes and tabs, sidebar controls, buttons, focus rings, links, task checkboxes, the heading minimap, and Find/Replace controls.

Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** independently under **Theme**. Each accent has colors suited to light and dark appearances. System follows your operating system's appearance while keeping your selected accent.

The color picker supports keyboard navigation. Tab to the selected color, then use the arrow keys to move between colors.

Existing settings automatically use Forest until another accent is chosen.

Verification covers every accent in light and dark mode, system appearance changes, keyboard navigation, app restarts, preserved preferences, invalid settings, and text/button contrast.
